## What's new in v0.16.0

### Video radio that follows your seed

- Artist radio now stays anchored to the artist you chose, with more videos from that artist and their closest related artists.
- Familiar favorites lead each mix while unfamiliar songs are blended in throughout, with stronger protection against repeated songs and overused artists.
- TIDAL relationships, Last.fm similar artists, and focused genre matches now expand the mix without letting broad genre results take over.

### Faster discovery from your library

- Artists from liked videos and favorite albums are indexed quietly in small background batches, including more videos from their catalogs and their related artists.
- Cached video catalogs and artist relationships make later radio sessions start and replenish faster while keeping external requests controlled.

### Smoother video sessions

- The selected seed video stays at the front of a new radio session instead of reappearing later in the queue.
- The related-video area now fits cleanly below the player and search results, with duplicate songs filtered from the session.
