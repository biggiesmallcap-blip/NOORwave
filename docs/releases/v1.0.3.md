## What's new in v1.0.3

A quick patch for playback hiccups. Retraining recommendations no longer freezes the player, and radio queues stop showing songs that will never load.

### Smoother playback while recommendations retrain

- Saving a retrain no longer locks up the app. Before, volume, pause and play could stop responding for about a minute, a DJ transition could leave the now-playing track looking stopped, and controls stayed sluggish for a while afterwards.
- Clearing out old recommendation data, after a retrain or at startup, now happens in small steps in the background, so the volume slider and transport controls stay responsive.

### Radio queues

- Songs a radio picks that can't be found on TIDAL are now removed from the queue instead of sitting on "Resolving on TIDAL..." forever. Songs you queued yourself are kept and tried again when they come up.
- When a radio tops itself up, the new songs are found on TIDAL right away instead of only once playback reaches them.

### Fixes

- The Discovery, Radio index, Database storage and Remove unused recommendations panels in Settings no longer snap shut on their own while a job is running.
