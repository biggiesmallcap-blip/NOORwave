## What's new in v1.0.2

A quick patch for radio. Stations now stay true to the song or artist you started them from, even for music your library barely knows, and Automix gets simpler to set up.

### Radio stays on its station

- A song, album or artist radio keeps adding from its own seed when it runs low, instead of handing over to Automix and drifting toward whatever you played recently.
- Radio for a song you have never played, with no genre or audio analysis yet, no longer fills up with one unrelated set of songs. When NOORwave knows too little about a song or artist, the station fills from TIDAL's own radio for it.
- After this update, NOORwave retrains its recommendations once more in the background at low CPU priority to clear out those unrelated matches.

### Simpler Automix

- One Include new switch replaces Include new, External picks and Learned radio. Learned picks are always used when they are available. Your current choice carries over.
- With Include new on, Last.fm picks take most of each top-up when the song playing is one your library knows little about, and a smaller share when it knows it well. Matches for the song are looked up as soon as it starts.

### Fixes

- The training progress bar in Settings no longer jumps backwards while a run saves its results, and every step has a readable name.
- Not for me shows once, on queue rows, with a choice between the song and everything by the artist, instead of twice on every menu.
