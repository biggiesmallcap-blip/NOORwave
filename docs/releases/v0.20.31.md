## What's new in v0.20.31

### Genre Galaxy

- The galaxy has a new look: a night sky, stars and labels drawn from your colour scheme, flat matte planets, and each sub-genre in its own shade of its family's colour. Light mode stays readable.
- Genre names are easier to read. Families get soft pills under their cluster, sub-genres get outlines, and labels that would overlap are hidden instead of piling up.
- Clicking a genre opens its details in a drawer inside the galaxy, so you keep your place. Expand shows the genre's full track list.
- Track counts in the galaxy now match the genre's own list.
- Hovering shows a single card, without the Mix pill that followed the cursor. The view refits when the window is resized.
- Removed a broken preview-tracks list from the genre panel and a dead TIDAL genres button.

### Videos

- Explore stations show up the day you update, instead of after the next day's lineup.
- Going fullscreen now switches in one clean dark frame, with no leftover title bar or white edges.

### Clean-up

- Removed an unused TIDAL Explore page that was never linked.
