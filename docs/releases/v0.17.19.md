## What's new in v0.17.19

### TIDAL sign-in

- The pending sign-in form now stays available when you return from your browser or navigate between Settings and setup. Your pasted redirect URL is kept until sign-in succeeds or you cancel.
- Failed sign-in attempts keep the form open for retry. Guidance explains how to copy the final browser URL even when TIDAL's redirect page shows a 404 error.

### Clay appearance

- Choose the new Clay colour scheme for warm parchment surfaces, clay accents, and dark ink. New installations start with light Clay and a calm background; saved appearance choices are preserved.
- Navigation, panels, menus, and players use coordinated colours on light and dark surfaces. Play and pause icons are centered consistently across desktop and phone controls.

### Video browsing and queues

- Shelf Play all now plays every video in the displayed order. Library video radio keeps its opening selection and adds discoveries to the queue.
- Move between Videos, Liked videos, and TIDAL editorial through shared navigation, with quick links back to picks or the player.
- Video menus offer Add to queue and Play next. Queue menus let you play a video, move it next, or remove it while keeping the current stream and position. Queue playback also works from Liked videos and TIDAL editorial.
