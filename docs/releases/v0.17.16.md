## What's new in v0.17.16

### A player that fits your workspace

- Choose whether the desktop player lives on the left, right, or bottom. Narrow windows temporarily use the bottom layout so playback controls and your music stay usable, then return to your saved side when space allows.
- The bottom player now has a purpose-built horizontal layout with artwork, metadata, transport, seeking, volume, and a full queue drawer. Opening the queue no longer takes playback controls away.
- Side players gain square or wide artwork choices, a clearer queue-focused view, denser queue rows, and more stable control placement across short and tall windows.
- Streaming quality can be hidden, shown as an icon, expanded into full sample-rate and bit-depth details, or displayed both ways independently for side and bottom players.

### Responsive shell and appearance

- The desktop shell, sidebar, library rows, search results, album pages, and playlist pages now adapt to the available workspace instead of assuming one wide window size.
- NOORwave remembers desktop window size and position across restarts, keeps restored windows on screen, and stores UI zoom separately from native window geometry.
- Dark, light, and system surface modes now have a live player preview in Appearance settings, while existing accents and wallpapers remain independently configurable.
- Player actions, favorite controls, artwork, metadata, and playback state stay aligned and synchronized as the shell changes layout or the audio runtime changes.

### Faster, more resilient artist pages

- Artist pages now show a fast preview first, then continue loading albums, singles, compilations, and other releases in background batches.
- Release sections use their own paginated filters, so large discographies can fill completely without making the first page wait for every TIDAL request.
- Slow or partial release loads now show accurate progress and retry controls instead of looking like an empty discography.

### Deeper video radio discovery

- Video radio now keeps expanding from the selected artist through related artists and focused genre matches, producing a broader queue without letting generic results take over.
- Familiar favorites still lead the mix, while unfamiliar finds are blended in with stronger protection against repeated songs and overused artists.
- New discoveries are surfaced as they are added to the queue, including which artists were found, and temporary discovery failures retry near the end of the queue.

### Reliability and maintenance

- Responsive player interactions and frontend contracts received additional coverage, including queue behavior, layout persistence, artist release loading, and video radio refills.
- A vulnerable transitive frontend dependency was patched, and an unused promotional build was removed from repository automation.
