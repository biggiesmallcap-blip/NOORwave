## What's new in v1.0.7

### Playlists

- Create regular playlists from the Playlists page, and switch between grid and list views. Tabs filter All, Favorites, TIDAL, Local and Smart.
- Removing tracks can be undone for a few seconds before anything changes on TIDAL.
- Add tracks from your library or TIDAL without leaving a playlist, filter and sort the shown rows, remove duplicates, and edit a playlist's name and description.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Delete removes the selection, Escape clears it, Ctrl+A selects all. Track menus gain Move to top and Move to bottom.
- Removing or moving tracks in a TIDAL playlist now always changes the right item on TIDAL, even when the playlist holds videos or was edited in another app.
- Selecting one copy of a repeated track no longer removes every copy, and a failed reorder snaps back.
- Add and save failures explain what went wrong, such as a track that is not on TIDAL.

### Radio

- Settings > Playback > Radio picks where stations come from (Balanced, Last.fm, TIDAL radio, or TIDAL + Last.fm) and how adventurous they are (Familiar, Mixed, Adventurous).
- A radio blind test in the same section lets you compare station designs by ear.
- Song Radio from a TIDAL track now runs the full station and keeps topping up the queue.
- Stations stay closer to the seed: weak genre guesses stay out, and album and artist radio spread picks across artists.
- A new release of a song you already know uses what NOORwave has learned about it.

### Playback and output

- Playback follows a new Windows default output, such as a headset connecting, instead of pausing.
- Fixed short gaps of silence during hi-res playback, and restored high-priority audio in shared mode.
- Play after an output fault resumes on the first press, and Resume after unplugging a device reopens the selected output.
- A track that fails to start is retried once, then reported, instead of retrying forever.
- Fixed a server freeze when DJ status and a track change overlapped, and cases where the queue and the playing track could fall out of step.
- Settings shows a clear label for a saved output device that is not connected.

### Library, popups and video

- Album quick view matches the album page, with track menus, queue and radio actions, and Play that toggles to Pause.
- Links inside popups open in the app instead of reloading it.
- Library landing artwork appears at once, mural refreshes crossfade, and random picks hold for an hour.
- TIDAL tracks that were withdrawn and re-released heal to the playable release, keeping plays and playlists.
- Genre matching no longer swaps genre words or matches place names, and New Wave is now its own genre.
- The app reconnects after a dropped connection to the server.
- Video: media keys work in fullscreen, Q opens the video queue, and a failed stream no longer reloads in a loop.
