## What's new in v0.19.52

### Since v0.19.47

- With the video queue open in a side layout, the video now plays in the panel's artwork slot instead of floating over the queue. Elsewhere the mini player stays clear of side panels and the player bar, snaps to a corner you drag it to (or pick with the arrow keys), remembers it, and can shrink to a pill while the video keeps playing.
- Video pages use the same centered navigation pills as Search and Library, and the way back to the playing video is the standard Back link at the top left.
- Stations has a wide spotlight hero and horizontal rails of station cards that name the artists.
- Liked videos link to the right artist. Some opened, and seeded radio from, an unrelated TIDAL artist; affected history is repaired so discovery stops chasing the wrong artists.
- Settings shows the installed version and update status again.

### Video stations and discovery

- Videos now has a Stations tab with a daily lineup, a rotating artist spotlight, and stations drawn from your taste and the indexed video catalog. Stations keep adding videos as you watch.
- Background discovery starts with videos from liked artists, then explores related and new artists. Video radio and related videos use those connections, with watched and enjoyed videos helping guide discovery.
- Choose Full, Limited or Off video discovery in Settings > Services > TIDAL. Limited uses about a tenth of the requests; Off stops background searches while keeping lookups for video radio you start.
- Stations show when their catalog is still being built and explain when there are not enough videos to play.

### DJ transitions and playback

- DJ transitions use the planned cues, timing and gain changes, with more blend, cut, bass-swap and energy-change choices when the audio analysis supports them. Conservative, Balanced and Adventurous preferences guide those choices, with safe crossfades when evidence is limited.
- DJ and Automix show the outgoing track, transition and incoming track together. Transition animation follows confirmed playback, and the cockpit keeps useful information visible when a request fails, with an inline retry.
- Seeking during or after a mix follows the incoming track's original audio and position, including paused seeks. Unavailable sources and failed analysis no longer trigger repeated retry loops.

### Playlists and library

- Playlist search finds user-made Spotify playlists and tries alternative sources when a search service is unavailable or returns no results. Found playlists can also open and save when the proxy is down.
- TIDAL catalogue replacements preserve the original library dates and track identity. Chosen dates and explicit favorite or unfavorite actions stay intact through replacement and sync.

### Security fixes

- Updated dependency components used for source maps, selector parsing and network probes with security fixes.
