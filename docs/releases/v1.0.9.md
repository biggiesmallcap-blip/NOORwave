## What's new in v1.0.9

### Spotify playlists

- Spotify playlists, albums and tracks are now labelled as external, with a Spotify badge, so it is clear where they come from.
- Save playlist is a proper button now. A saved playlist remembers the Spotify playlist it came from, and the page links to your copy.
- Update from Spotify adds the songs added on Spotify since you saved it. Songs you removed or moved in your copy stay that way, and saving again no longer makes a duplicate.
- Rescan unmatched looks for songs on TIDAL again when they had no match or only a doubtful one.

### Browsing

- Personal Radio stations, TIDAL playlists in search results and playlists in a Discover "View all" page open their track list instead of starting to play. The play button on the cover still plays straight away.
- With mouse-wheel shelf scrolling on, the wheel no longer gets stuck on a shelf. A shelf only takes the wheel after you point at it, and hands it back to the page at its end.
