## What's new in v1.0.1

The first patch after 1.0 is about what plays next. Radio, Automix, DJ and Sound Space now share one set of rules for picking songs, learn from your skips more fairly, and give you a way to say no. The phone remote also got a proper rework.

### Not for me

- New Not for me option on every track and artist menu. Marked songs and artists stay out of radio, Automix, recommendations, Sound Space and blends.
- Rejecting the song that is playing now moves on to the next one instead of stopping playback.

### Better picks in radio and Automix

- Every recommendation source passes the same check, so recently played songs, early skips, hidden tracks, Not for me and duplicate versions of a song are kept out everywhere.
- Only skips in the first moments of a song count against it. Songs you let play partway are neutral, and a skip is forgotten once you play the song through.
- Automix refills stay close to the station's seed or the song you picked, instead of drifting off.
- Automix finds new music through Last.fm links instead of a broad search, and skips a pick it cannot play instead of stopping.
- Relevance comes first in DJ and Automix queues. Key and tempo fit only nudge the order while mixing, using one consistent rule for harmonic and tempo matches.
- Adventurous radio and blends reach further into related artists. Songs people often play together count for more than raw play totals.
- Radio quality settings are now on for everyone.

### Sound Space

- The starting coherence adapts to your recent likes and skips.
- Liking songs leans the session toward their energy and tempo.
- Genre branches match the actual genre, not any genre that shares a word.

### Recommendations learn in the background

- After this update, NOORwave retrains its recommendations once in the background at low CPU priority. A one-time notice tells you, with a Run now button if you want it done at full speed. Closing the app mid-run does not use up the attempt.
- A newly trained model is used whenever it beats the current one.
- The trainer panel in Settings reads in plain words: the last run says Finished, Stopped or Interrupted, and the active model and CPU profile are easier to follow.

### Phone remote

- Phones show a real name when paired, like iPhone - Safari, instead of all reading Phone remote.
- The pairing QR closes as soon as your phone connects. An expired code blurs and offers a new one.
- The device list shows which phones are connected right now and when the others were last seen.
- Rename, revoke and reset now confirm inline instead of in browser popups.
- New PIN sign-in setting under Settings > Phone remote. In the desktop app, signing in from another device with the shared PIN is off until you turn it on. New PIN changes the PIN without unpairing your phones.
- The remote no longer drops and reconnects on phone Wi-Fi, and it catches up immediately when you unlock your phone.
- Media keys and the tray keep working after the PIN changes.

### Fixes

- Pictures that are not cached yet load straight from TIDAL, so a page of fresh search results, video search especially, no longer shows blank tiles.
- Turning the artwork cache off stops downloads that were already waiting.
- Liked songs no longer get stuck showing Saved locally. Songs already stuck are repaired on startup.
