## What's new in v0.20.30

### Video stations

- Stations is now a channel guide. Each numbered row shows a filmstrip of what the station would play: click a frame to start the station from that video, hover one to see its name, and the playing station is marked On air. The spotlight is channel 01, with the artist's portrait, why they were picked and a short bio.
- A new Explore group adds stations for Latin, Reggaeton, Reggae and dancehall, Afrobeats, K-pop, Metal, Punk, Classical, Jazz, Country and Americana, and Disco and funk. Scenes that repeat one of your genre stations are skipped. Turn the row or any scene off in Settings > Services > Explore stations.
- The Videos tab uses the same calmer guide layout: Daily picks leads with Play all and Start video radio, and each library shelf is a simple row with its name and count. Editorial picks stay on their own tab.
- Switching between Videos and Stations no longer pops content in. Both tabs keep what they last showed and ease in like Home and Library.

### Watching videos

- Related videos fill two full rows of close picks, then keep going below with more, coming into focus as you scroll.
- Going fullscreen no longer flashes the window's minimize, maximize and close buttons over the video. The window goes fullscreen first, then the video grows to fill it.
