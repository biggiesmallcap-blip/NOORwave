## What's new in v1.0.0

NOORwave 1.0, the hundredth release. Six months to the day after the first commit, the player has a single look and feel on every page. This release is that pass: one design system, one way to lay out a page, and one action bar on every album, artist and playlist.

Thank you to everyone who installed an early build, sent a screenshot of something broken, or just kept listening. 1.0 is yours too.

### NOORwave 1.0 at a glance

- Your TIDAL library in a local database, so search returns results as you type.
- Gapless lossless playback, with WASAPI exclusive output on Windows.
- Planned DJ transitions and an automix that learns from your own library.
- Every music video for the songs you love, indexed into one wall, plus video stations with a fresh lineup every day.
- Genre Galaxy: your whole library drawn as a star field you can fly through.
- Sound Space for discovery that branches from any song.
- Listening analytics, Last.fm scrobbling, themes, animated backgrounds, and your phone as the remote.

### A new look, everywhere

- Pages sit directly on the background instead of inside boxed panels. Settings, stat tiles, empty states and route panels lost their frames.
- Every page shares the same edge and the same title size. Each section has one title, with no small label stacked above it.
- Album, artist and playlist pages, Spotify pages and the album quick view all share one action bar with Play, Shuffle and the rest in the same places.
- Artist pages use a new Stage layout: counts appear once, the discography is split into tabs, and every row shows its duration.
- Album pages read like liner notes: tracks grouped by work, one action bar, and no repeated details.
- Loading pages show a skeleton after a short pause instead of a spinner, so fast loads no longer flash.
- Errors are written in plain language and come with a Retry button. An empty library search offers to search TIDAL instead.

### Finding your way

- The sidebar is grouped by job: Listen, Explore and Tools. Sound Space is now named to match its page.
- Windows between 680 and 899 pixels wide get a compact icon sidebar.
- Wide windows and smaller interface sizes are handled better: rows fill the width, columns line up, and Settings search stays next to its form.
- Home opens with Jump back in, and the shelves below it are reordered.

### Search

- The top result sits beside the five best songs. Songs already in your library are marked with a ring.
- Pressing Enter plays the top artist without leaving the results.
- The keyboard cursor stays in view as you move through results.
- The suggestion dropdown under search fields is gone.

### Library

- A new header with tab counts and a toolbar that stays in place.
- Songs lists your liked songs. A setting shows every song in your library instead.
- Song rows show quality only when it is unusual, and their actions appear on hover.

### Mix

- Automix and DJ are now one Mix page.
- Queue rows are flat and show a single status. The DJ notice appears inline, and reasons are written out in words.
- Crossfade and the default transition style moved to Settings > Playback.

### Videos

- New setting for the fullscreen transition, with adjustable grow speed and dim length. Going fullscreen is quicker.
- Native fullscreen in the desktop app switches in a single step and keeps the window in front.
- The Keep exploring row under a video no longer waits for the radio station to finish building.

### Comfort

- New Reduce motion setting under Settings > Appearance, applied across the whole app.
- Artwork is no longer dimmed in light themes, and captions on the side rail are no longer cut off.
- Arrow keys on tabs and toggles no longer seek playback.

### Under the hood

- Releases are tagged with a message, so signed-tag setups no longer stop a release.

### By the numbers

- 6 months since the first commit on April 9, 2026.
- 1,944 commits and 254 merged pull requests.
- 100 releases: this is the hundredth.

Here's to the next hundred.
