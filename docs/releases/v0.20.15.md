## What's new in v0.20.15

### Videos

- Videos has one watch page with a shared header across the video tabs. It opens at the top, keeps a single queue beside the player with no gap underneath, and Back returns you to where you were scrolled in the tab you came from.
- The player glides smoothly between the watch page and its corner spot instead of jumping or stuttering, and the glide starts only once the video is showing its first frame.
- Shrinking the player to a pill no longer resizes the video, and growing it back onto the watch page is no longer clipped. The title stays clear of the bottom bar.
- Entering and leaving fullscreen is smooth, with the glide and the window resize no longer overlapping.
- Scrolling with the mouse wheel over the video scrolls the page, with eased motion.
