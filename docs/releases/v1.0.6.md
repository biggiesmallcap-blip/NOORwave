## What's new in v1.0.6

### Playlists and mixes

- Open a TIDAL mix or discovery playlist to browse its tracks without starting playback. Use the separate play control when you are ready to listen.
- Save the displayed track list as a TIDAL playlist with a linked NOORwave copy, preserving the order and repeated tracks.
- Add tracks to playlists from artist pages, discovery results and the queue. Linked playlists update on TIDAL before the local copy changes.
- Refresh linked playlists from TIDAL to pick up edits. Failed syncs remain eligible for retry.
- Play a queued track without replacing the queue, and reorder repeated playlist entries independently.

### Library and artwork

- Keep Library landing content visible while other lists load, including when returning to the page or refreshing.
- Load top artist artwork promptly and reach an available cover sooner when a portrait fails.

### Playback and output

- Improve recovery from output-device, decoder and stream failures, and prevent older playback sessions from interrupting the current track.
- Improve resampler continuity at the end of a stream and reduce audio-buffer contention.
- Show the actual output sample rate in the player, with the source resolution in the tooltip, including when reopening the app during playback.
- Avoid repeated exclusive-mode warnings and keep saved output-device selections clear when a device is unavailable.
