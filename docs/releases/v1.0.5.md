## What's new in v1.0.5

The Library page opens instantly. Your top artists, Recently Added and suggestions show up at once instead of loading every time.

### Library

- Your top 20 artists appear as soon as the page opens, then update quietly in the background.
- Suggested tracks and albums show their covers straight away instead of filling in tile by tile.
- Recently Added always shows your newest albums, whatever sort or search you last used on the Songs tab.

### Updates

- Patch notes in the update dialog now show proper headings and lists instead of raw text.
