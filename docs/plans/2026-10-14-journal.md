# Journal: plan sketch

Date: 2026-10-14. Status: sketch, not started. A fifth video tab (after On
Air, Atlas, Archive and Logbook) that grows the Today pieces into a small
magazine about the listener's own catalog. Same rule as Today: facts only
(the catalog, the listener's history, TIDAL or Last.fm biographies,
Wikidata origins), written in fixed sentences. No generated prose, no LLM.

## Where it lives

- Journal tab: the full magazine.
- On Air keeps a block below Scenes. Open: either today's three pieces as
  they are now, or a slim "From the Journal" strip (today's three pictures
  and names, linking into the tab).

## First version (core)

### 1. Today

The three daily pieces (today's Spotlight artist, someone new, someone from
the archive or elsewhere on the map), set larger, as a front page. Built by
`services/video_air/today.rs` and `server/routes/video_today_routes.rs` as
now.

### 2. Artist of the week

- Chosen once per ISO week and stored, so it holds all week:
  `server_config` key `video_air.journal.week:{yyyy-Www}`.
- Needs plenty to say: about 20 or more videos across several years, some
  live videos or concerts, some collaborators. Never the same artist within
  a year; a different scene each week.
- The feature: a large still, the name, a few paragraphs from the Today
  writer (a new feature kind in `today.rs`), then:
  - a timeline: one picture per year they made videos (song year when
    trusted, else upload year);
  - their concerts and live cuts;
  - who they are on video with (`journey::collaborators`);
  - where they sit on the map and their neighbours;
  - their biography and origin (the Today enrichment cache).
- Endpoint: `GET /api/videos/journal/week`.

### 3. The Wall

Every artist with a picture as one mosaic.

- Tiles: a triangle lattice with each vertex nudged a little so it reads
  hand-laid. Bigger artists take two triangles joined into a diamond, or
  six into a hexagon; tiles share edges, no gaps, thin dark joints like
  grout.
- Order: by hue, then lightness, so the wall flows in colour bands.
- Load: each tile paints its colour at once; the picture (160 px) fills in
  as the tile comes into view.
- Hover shows the name and video count; a click plays their best cuts.
- Client only, on Canvas 2D: the Atlas's Picture data
  (`atlasPictures`: ids, rgb, art) plus the map's names and video counts.
  No new endpoint.

### 4. Back issues

- Each day's Today is stored the first time it is made:
  `server_config` key `video_air.journal.issue:{yyyy-mm-dd}`.
- `GET /api/videos/journal?before={day}&limit={n}` returns earlier issues;
  the tab scrolls back through them like a blog. Days the app was not
  opened have no issue.

## Server work

- Store each day's issue; the issues endpoint.
- Weekly pick and store; the week's feature endpoint (timeline, concerts,
  collaborators, neighbours, biography, origin).
- Reuse what is cached: the Today enrichment (biographies, Wikidata), the
  artist graph, the map facts.

## Later

Tracked in `FOLLOWUPS.md` ("videos: Journal, after the core"):

- Collages: One song, every cut (the most-filmed songs, their stills side
  by side); A year in pictures; On stage (a concert wall).
- Short stories: New on the map this week; The most collaborative artist
  in a scene; Songs filmed 10 times; Your month in videos.

## Rough size

About half a day for the server side, half a day for the Wall, a few hours
for the page and the On Air block.
